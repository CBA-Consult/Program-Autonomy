### **Portfolio Level Objective: The SCEV Customer Experience (CX) Initiative**

*   **Title:** Revolutionize Automotive Ownership with SCEV CX
*   **Description:** Deliver an intelligent, self-charging electric vehicle ecosystem that provides true energy independence and unparalleled customer satisfaction, eliminating core EV ownership friction points like range anxiety and charging dependency. This initiative governs a portfolio of interconnected programs and projects.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Achieve Net Promoter Score (NPS) ≥ 72.
    *   Capture ≥16% market share in target premium EV segments.
    *   Achieve a positive Return on Investment (ROI) within 8 years of market entry.
*   **Resource Estimates:** $735 million over 11 years (Years 1-11)

### **Program Level Initiatives**

#### **1. Program Genesis (The Core Vehicle)**

*   **Title:** Develop Foundational SCEV Platform
*   **Description:** Deliver the foundational, high-quality vehicle platform, engineered for safety and scalability, which will serve as the base for integrating all self-charging energy systems and digital experiences.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Commercial launch of the MVP vehicle ≤ 7 years from program start.
    *   Vehicle platform meets all defined safety, performance, and scalability engineering standards.
*   **Resource Estimates:** Primarily funded in Phase 2 (Years 4-7) and Phase 3 (Years 8-11) – part of $680M total for experience-level programs.

    #### **Project Level Deliverable: Project Bedrock (Scalable Chassis Integration)**

    *   **Title:** Develop Modular "Skateboard" Chassis
    *   **Description:** Design and develop a modular, scalable "skateboard" chassis that provides a flexible physical platform, enabling seamless compatibility with all energy capture systems and supporting rapid prototyping and manufacturing across the SCEV portfolio.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:**
        *   100% compatibility achieved with all planned energy systems (solar, kinetic, maglev).
        *   Chassis design validated for rapid prototyping and efficient manufacturing processes.
    *   **Resource Estimates:** Primarily funded in Phase 1 (Years 1-3) – part of $55M total for R&D projects.

        ##### **Task Level Work Items under Project Bedrock:**

        **1.1.1. Chassis Architecture & Design**
        *   **Title:** Define Scalable Chassis Architecture
        *   **Description:** Develop the core modular "skateboard" chassis architecture, including structural components, material specifications, and layout to accommodate various energy capture systems and vehicle configurations.
        *   **Acceptance Criteria:**
            *   Detailed architectural specifications and engineering drawings approved.
            *   Modularity confirmed through design reviews for future vehicle variants.
        *   **Resource Estimates:** 6-9 months, 3-5 mechanical/structural engineers.

        **1.1.2. Prototype Development & Validation**
        *   **Title:** Build & Validate Chassis Prototype
        *   **Description:** Fabricate initial chassis prototypes using selected materials. Conduct rigorous structural integrity, crash safety, and load-bearing capacity tests.
        *   **Acceptance Criteria:**
            *   Functional chassis prototype built and tested.
            *   Test reports confirm structural integrity and safety compliance.
        *   **Resource Estimates:** 9-12 months, 4-6 engineers/technicians.

        **1.1.3. Energy System Interface Design**
        *   **Title:** Design Energy System Integration Interfaces
        *   **Description:** Define and design the physical, electrical, and data interfaces on the chassis for seamless integration of solar, kinetic, and in-wheel induction (maglev) energy capture systems.
        *   **Acceptance Criteria:**
            *   Interface specifications documented and approved by all relevant energy project teams.
            *   Physical mock-ups demonstrate successful compatibility and fit.
        *   **Resource Estimates:** 4-6 months, 2-3 integration engineers.

#### **2. Project Autonomy (The Energy Experience)**
