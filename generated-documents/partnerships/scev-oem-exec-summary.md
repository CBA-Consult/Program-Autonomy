# SCEV — Executive Summary for OEM Partnership

Purpose
- Introduce SCEV's self-charging vehicle technology and propose a strategic partnership for pilot and mass-production readiness with a high-volume OEM.

What we offer
- Core technology: Integrated self-charging platform (photovoltaics, kinetic harvest, smart BMS, wireless inductive charging options).
- Differentiated value: Energy autonomy for end users (reduced lifecycle energy cost and ability to export energy to the grid), modular systems designed for retrofit and OEM integration, and measurable sustainability KPIs.

Why partner with SCEV
- Product differentiation: Add energy-autonomy features to OEM model lines for a unique market position vs competitors.
- Customer economics: Lower total cost of ownership via lifecycle energy reductions and potential V2G revenues for end customers—improves resale value and brand sustainability credentials.
- Faster innovation: Co-development reduces R&D duplication and accelerates time-to-market for energy-autonomy features.
- Risk sharing: Pilot-first approach with phased scaling reduces manufacturing and warranty exposure.

Proposed partnership model (high level)
1. Phase 0 — NDA & feasibility (60 days): Technical due diligence, IP review, manufacturing fit assessment.
2. Phase 1 — Joint pilot (6–12 months): 20–100 pilot vehicles (fleet + consumer pilot), performance validation, safety & EMC testing, production engineering feedback.
3. Phase 2 — Production readiness (12–24 months): Supplier qualification, APQP/PPAP, tooling, quality plans, and launch to tiered volume production.
4. Phase 3 — High-volume supply & licensing: Supply agreement, licensing, or JV for mass roll-out with agreed pricing, warranty, and service models.

Commercial ask
- Pilot co-funding and access to a pilot fleet and manufacturing engineers for integration validation.
- Access to OEM calibration labs and EMC facilities.

Key success metrics
- Pilot: Energy harvested per vehicle per day; charge autonomy % (vehicles that reach target SOC without plug-in); safety and EMC compliance pass rate.
- Production: APQP milestone completion, PPAP approval, supplier readiness, and target per-unit manufacturing cost.

Quick wins for OEM
- Demonstrable sustainability story to support regulatory compliance and PR.
- New value proposition to fleet customers (reduced operating cost + grid services).
- Low-risk pilot demonstrated in real-world conditions within 6–12 months.

Next steps

- Share preliminary integration documents (BOM, mechanical interfaces, electrical specs, EMC requirements).
