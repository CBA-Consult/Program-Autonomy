# SCEV — Pilot Proposal

Overview
- Objective: Validate self-charging system integration, performance, and user experience in real-world conditions to de-risk production readiness.
- Duration: 6–12 months (typical) depending on scope.

Scope
- Vehicle integration: Fitment on representative chassis (EV fleet or consumer model), including mechanical mounting, wiring harnesses, and BMS/ECU integration.
- Testing: Energy harvest validation, charge autonomy metrics, thermal performance, safety, EMC, and durability under operational profiles.
- Data collection: Telemetry for energy flows, state-of-charge (SoC), consumption, ambient conditions, and event logs.
- User trials: Fleet operator and consumer trials to collect UX feedback and refine control strategies.

Deliverables
- Integration package: BOM, mechanical drawings, wiring harness diagrams, and ECU calibration notes.
- Test report: Performance vs. target KPIs, EMC results, thermal/durability notes, and remediation plans.
- Software artifacts: BMS/ECU calibration profiles, logging/telemetry stack, and cloud ingestion pipeline (if applicable).
- Acceptance criteria: Pre-agreed KPI thresholds (e.g., minimum daily energy harvested, no critical EMC failures, successful thermal soak testing).

Responsibilities
- SCEV: Deliver hardware kits, integration guidance, data collection software, and engineering support.
- OEM: Provide vehicles, lab/facilities access, calibration/test engineers, and safety sign-off authority for on-road trials.

Data & IP
- Data sharing: Pilot telemetry will be shared under agreed confidentiality and used for joint analysis and product improvement.
- IP: Existing SCEV IP remains owned by SCEV; joint developments may be subject to a separate joint-ownership agreement or license.

Timeline (example)

- Weeks 8–24: Integration, lab testing, and initial trials.
- Weeks 24–48: Extended field trials, data analysis, and production readiness planning.

Acceptance & Handover
- Acceptance: Pilot considered successful when agreed KPIs met and APQP handover materials are delivered.
- Handover: Transition to production-engineering phase with supplier/quality documentation.
