# Battery Supply Risk Dashboard — Draft (one-pager)

Status: draft — quick first-pass to operationalize supply-chain exposure. Owner: PMO Program Manager (Menno Drescher)

Purpose
- Provide PMO and execs a concise heatmap of the top battery-supply dependencies, current mitigations, and recommended immediate actions.

Summary heatmap

- Highest overall risk (score 9): R1 Raw materials (mining & refining), R2 Cell manufacturing, R10 Financing.
- High risks (score 7): R3 Electrode & precursor supply, R8 Pricing & demand.

Top 5 dependencies and recommended actions

1) R1 — Mining & refining (Li/Ni/Co) — Overall risk 9
   - Exposure: high. EU lacks refining scale; China dominates.
   - Current mitigation: monitoring and spot purchases.
   - Recommended action (2w): Pre-qualify alternate refiners; start discussions with EU/US refineries and specialist traders. Owner: Head of Supply Chain.

2) R2 — Cell manufacturing capacity — Overall risk 9
   - Exposure: high; EU capacity insufficient and CAPEX-intensive.
   - Current mitigation: relationship-building with Northvolt and other players.
   - Recommended action (4w): Explore JV / tolling / off-take term sheets with potential fabs; engage Head of Partnerships to convene term-sheet discussions. Owner: Head of Partnerships.

3) R10 — Financing for onshoring — Overall risk 9
   - Exposure: high; CAPEX and investor caution.
   - Recommended action (6-12w): Draft co-investment models and approach EIB/EC financing; prepare robust project CBA. Owner: CFO / PMO Finance Sponsor.

4) R3 — Electrode & precursor supplies — Overall risk 7
   - Recommended action (4w): Qualify alternate suppliers and implement partial inventory hedging. Owner: Procurement Lead.

5) R8 — BEV pricing & demand — Overall risk 7
   - Recommended action (3w): Accelerate low-cost BEV pilots and produce a TCO sensitivity analysis. Owner: Product Lead.
