# Roadmap & Gates — Two-lane Approach

This draft sets out two conditional roadmap lanes to keep Program Autonomy aligned to policy/regulatory outcomes and market timing.

Lane A — Accelerate (2035 confirmed)
- Objective: Prioritize electrification, accelerate pilot-to-production activities, secure battery supply agreements, and scale V2G/V2H grid services.
- Key gates:
  - Gate A1 (Q4 2025): Battery supply risk dashboard complete; 1 strategic off-take or partnership term sheet signed.
  - Gate A2 (Q2 2026): Pilot scale readiness (50 vehicles/homes); certifications scoped and lab bookings scheduled.
  - Gate A3 (Q4 2026): Production readiness decision; supply contracts and financing committed.
- Primary KPIs: secured cell off-take capacity (kWh), pilot readiness score, projected unit economics vs ICE parity.

Lane B — Gradual (2035 relaxed)
- Objective: Pace investments, focus on profitable low-cost BEV variants and commercial customers, maintain optionality for full electrification.
- Key gates:
  - Gate B1 (Q1 2026): Policy-watch signals evaluated; roadmap adjustments made based on policy triggers.
  - Gate B2 (Q3 2026): Strategic pilots for grid services and low-cost vehicle variants; prepare APQP inputs for suppliers.
  - Gate B3 (Q1 2027): Reassess production cadence vs demand and supplier commitments.
- Primary KPIs: breakeven TCO for low-cost variants, pilot ROI, procurement cycle time.
